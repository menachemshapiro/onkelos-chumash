# Word-by-word alignment for "as Onkelos reads it", Bereishit 49.
# Each pasuk: "@ c:v", then per clause:  E: the English (<b>…</b> = Onkelos's wording)
#   followed by one line per Targum word or phrase:  ARAMAIC = english words used for it  (empty = not translated, e.g. יָת)
#   "+ words" = English words with no Aramaic word of their own (for English grammar), listed so nothing is unaccounted for.
#   "! note" = a remark for review.
# Clauses that need no change are written "= same".
# {…} in the English = a word we added that is in neither Metsudah nor (as a word of its own) the Targum, only where it makes the English read better.
